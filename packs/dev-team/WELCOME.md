# ✅ Your AI Dev Team is installed

You've added **Manny** and his team to Prime Agent Desk. You don't need to open or
edit anything in here — just talk to Manny in the app and he'll take it from there.

## Who's on your team
Manny leads these specialists and hands your project down the line, explaining each
step in plain language:

- **The Architect** — plans how your app should be built before anyone writes code.
- **The Designer** — decides how it looks and feels (screens, buttons, states).
- **The Builder** — writes the actual working software.
- **The Inspector** — tries hard to break it and reports every flaw to fix.
- **The Reporter** — gives you a short, plain-English status update when you ask.
- Plus a **Smoke Tester** and **Code Reviewer** Manny calls in when useful.

They also share a set of professional working habits (planning, careful debugging,
checking their work before saying it's done) that switch on automatically.

## How to use it
1. In Prime Agent Desk, make sure the folder selector at the top says **Manny**.
2. Tell Manny what you want to build — in your own words.
3. He'll walk you through it and build it with the team.

## "I only see this file — where's everything else?"
That's normal. The team's working files live in a folder called `.claude` that your
computer keeps **hidden** by default (that's just how technical support files work).
Everything is there and running — you interact with the team by chatting with Manny,
not by opening files. On a Mac you can press **Cmd + Shift + .** in Finder to reveal
hidden files if you're curious.
