# Living Avatar Template

Use this exact structure for `Avatar/Living Avatar.md`. Every claim line uses the claim format below. Sections stay in this order. Empty sections stay in the file with "*No confirmed patterns yet.*" so gaps are visible.

## Claim format

Each claim is a bolded one-line statement in customer-derived language, followed by its evidence block:

```
**[Claim in one line]**
Evidence: [N] distinct customers | First seen: [date] | Last seen: [date] | Strength: [Strong 5+ / Confirmed 3-4 / Provisional <3]
> "[verbatim quote]" — [ID], [source type], [date]
> "[verbatim quote]" — [ID], [source type], [date]
```

2 to 5 quotes per claim. Rank claims within each section by distinct-customer count, highest first.

## Document structure

```
# Living Avatar — [Business Name]
Version [X.Y] | Last updated [date] | Built from [N] sources, [N] distinct customers, [date range]

## Who they are
Identity language. How they describe themselves, their role, their self-image.

## Top pains
Ranked. The problem in their words, present tense.

## What it's costing them
Specific costs in their units: hours, money, sleep, relationships, credibility.

## Trigger events
The moments that made them seek help. Hook inventory.

## What they tried before
Failed alternatives. Competitor and category positioning.

## What they want
Desired outcomes, ranked. Include the non-obvious wants.

## Objections and hesitations
Exact hesitation sentences. Pre-handle these in webinars, FAQs, email sequences.

## Why they buy
Decision language. What tipped them.

## Sticky phrases / hook bank
Coined phrases and metaphors, each with source. No threshold required.

## History (retired claims)
Claims retired for staleness, with retirement date and original evidence block. Never delete; markets cycle.
```

## Version numbering

- Bootstrap = 1.0
- Any approved claim addition, update, or retirement = minor bump (1.1, 1.2)
- A structural change like a segment decision or full re-bootstrap = major bump (2.0)

Log every bump in `Avatar/Avatar Changelog.md`.
